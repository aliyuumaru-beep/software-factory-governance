## Department Sections

> **Column definitions:**
>
> - **Decision ID** — The unique identifier from `docs/DECISION_LOG.md` (e.g., DEC-001, DEC-OCA-02).
> - **Work Package** — The Work Package in which this decision was taken (e.g., WP-01) or programme context (e.g., M-C Governance Recovery).
> - **Approval Type** — One of: Sponsor Decision · Governance Decision · Architecture Decision · Exception · Escalation · Execution Authority (AOP-013) · Client Decision.
> - **Decision Taken** — Concise executive language. What was decided. Not what was discussed.
> - **Business Rationale** — WHY the decision was made, not WHAT changed.
> - **Approved By** — Name and role of the approving authority.
> - **Authority Class** — AOP-013 Execution Authority class applied at implementation (EA-1 through EA-6). Populate when AOP-013 is active; use "Pre-AOP-013" for legacy entries.
> - **Status** — Active · Deferred · Open · Closed · Revoked · Superseded.
> - **Date** — Date decision was taken (YYYY-MM-DD).
> - **Source Document** — Primary source document (Decision Log entry, Exit Gate Report, Governance Review, etc.).
